Hidden capability, not chrome, **not** the P0 hero or a default/demo Project.

remain `Requires-environment` / `not-run`. Same-disk Linux/CI evidence does

2. Rights-safe **original** content with `original-owner-rights`.
3. Daemon-issued digest-bound publish preview.
4. HITL confirm on the owner management channel (canvas, not chat Approve).
5. Persist-before-dispatch publish ledger (Intent row before `dispatched`).
6. Readback is honest `unknown` until a qualified live observation exists.
7. Fail-closed: evasion, raw secret, publish-without-confirm, receipt-as-completion, unknown metrics as 0, scraped content, X-as-P0-success-path.
